Benefits

Manage your vineyard smarter

ElixirLine gives you full control over your artisanal wine production. Coordinate tasks, log progress, and oversee your batches in real time with a tool built for rural settings.

📅 Structured Planning

Create and organize farming tasks by batch or plot. View your activity calendar and avoid miscoordination.

📲 Easy Field Logging

Report tasks with one tap—even offline. Upload photos, comments, and update your digital logbook easily.

📈 Stage-Based Monitoring

Track production at every stage: from fermentation to bottling. Spot deviations and improve quality.

📦 Input Control

Maintain an accurate and up-to-date inventory. Link materials to tasks or batches and get alerts when stock runs low.

🔔 Alerts and Communication

Notify your team of tasks, updates, and news instantly. Enhance coordination without face-to-face meetings.

👥 Interface for All Roles

The winemaker manages, the worker executes—both supported by an app designed for use in the field with no tech skills required.

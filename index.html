Benefits

Manage your vineyard smarter

ElixirLine gives you full control over your artisanal winemaking process. Coordinate tasks, log progress, and monitor your production in real time with a tool designed for rural environments.

📅 Structured Planning

Create and organize agricultural tasks by plot or batch. Visualize the task calendar and avoid miscommunication.

📲 Easy Field Logging

Report tasks with a single tap—even offline. Add photos, comments, and complete your digital logbook without hassle.

📈 Stage-by-Stage Supervision

Monitor production in every phase—from fermentation to bottling. Identify deviations and improve quality.

📦 Inventory Control

Keep an up-to-date inventory. Link materials to tasks or batches and get notified when stock is low.

🔔 Alerts & Communication

Notify your team of tasks, updates, and news instantly. Improve coordination without depending on in-person meetings.

👥 Interface for All Users

Producers manage, workers execute, and both collaborate with an app designed for simple use in the field—even with no technical background.
